=========================================
TRAVELER PROFILE & PREFERENCES
=========================================

1. GENERAL TRAVEL STYLE
- Travel Pace: Moderate (prefers 2-3 main activities per day; no rushing).
- Wake-Up / Start Time: Around 9:00 AM.
- Bedtime / Wind-Down: Around 10:30 PM.
- Pacing Rule: Always include a 1.5-hour afternoon coffee/rest break around 2:30 PM.

2. ACCOMMODATION & LOCATION PREFERENCES
- Preferred Neighborhoods: Quiet, walkable areas close to local transit hubs.
- Lodging Type: Boutique hotels or clean, highly-rated local apartments.
- Noise Sensitivity: High (avoid party districts or noisy main avenues).

3. DIETARY & DINING PREFERENCES
- Dietary Restrictions: None / [e.g., Vegetarian / Gluten-Free / Halal / Pescatarian].
- Food Interests: Authentic local cuisine, street food markets, hidden hole-in-the-wall spots, and cozy bakeries.
- Exclusions: Avoid tourist traps, large global fast-food chains, and overly formal fine dining requiring strict dress codes.
- Meal Budget Allocation: Splurge on dinners; keep breakfasts and lunches casual and budget-friendly.

4. ACTIVITY & CULTURAL PREFERENCES
- Primary Interests: Modern architecture, historic temples, quiet gardens, art galleries, and local craft markets.
- Dislikes / Skip List: Avoid crowded theme parks, long lines for overly commercial tourist spots, and intense high-cardio hikes.
- Walking Tolerance: Up to 10,000–12,000 steps per day, provided there are seating areas along the route.

5. BUDGET & FINANCIAL RULES
- Target Daily Budget: Moderate ($200–$300 USD / day including food, local transit, and activities; accommodation tracked separately).
- Payment Mode: Prefers using credit cards, but requires having enough local cash on hand for small vendors and street markets.
- Value Focus: Willing to pay extra for unique cultural experiences, but conservative on transport (prefers subways/trains over expensive taxis).

6. MOBILITY & LOGISTICS
- Preferred Transit: Public transportation (subway, trains, trams) and walking. Taxis/Rideshares reserved for late evenings or heavy luggage transfers.
- Airport Transfer: Prefers express airport trains or pre-booked private transfers over buses.
